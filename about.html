About

We learned this on the floor, not in a course

StockLedger exists because inventory businesses keep hiring two people who never speak to each other: a bookkeeper who has never walked a warehouse, and a warehouse manager who has never closed a month. We are both, in one team.

Between us we have spent over two decades inside businesses that carry stock — footwear and leather manufacturing, grocery e-commerce, food service, and export. Not advising them. Running them.

That means relocating live warehouses without losing inventory. Building a central hub that supplies nine sites. Holding pick accuracy at 900 orders a day. Setting up inventory inside Dynamics 365 and reconciling it back to the ledger. Building the cost model that finally tells an owner what a unit actually costs to make.

When we looked at what small businesses in the US, UK and the Gulf were being offered remotely, it was almost always one half of the picture. Bookkeepers who treat inventory as a single line item. Data entry providers with no idea what the numbers mean. Consultants who deliver a slide deck and leave.

So we built the other thing: one team that handles the stock, the books, and the cost — and reconciles all three against each other. That's the whole business.

Our position on honesty. We do not publish invented testimonials or borrowed logos. Every case study on this site is work our team personally led, described accurately, with client names withheld under confidentiality. References from the managers we reported to are available on request.
