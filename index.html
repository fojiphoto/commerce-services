The Real Problem

If you carry stock, a general bookkeeper isn't enough

Three things quietly drain profit in every inventory business. None of them show up on a standard P&L.

The count never matches

Your system says 1,400 units. The floor says 1,082. Nobody knows when the gap opened, so it gets written off every quarter and repeats the next one.

"We just adjust it at year end."

You don't know your true unit cost

Material, labour, freight, overhead, wastage — most owners price off a rough multiple. Some of your best-selling lines are losing money and you can't see which.

"We add 40% and hope."

Your books are 4 months behind

Uncategorised transactions, unreconciled bank feeds, inventory sitting as one lump expense. When you need a lender, a buyer, or a decision — the numbers aren't there.

"We'll clean it up before tax season."

Questions

Before you book

Which countries do you work with?

We work with businesses in the United States, United Kingdom, Canada, and the Middle East (UAE, Saudi Arabia, Qatar). Our team overlaps with US Eastern and UK business hours, and we're fully available during Gulf hours.

Do you only work in QuickBooks?

QuickBooks Online and Desktop are our primary platforms. We also work in Microsoft Dynamics 365, other ERP inventory modules, and Excel/Google Sheets. If you're on Xero or Zoho, tell us on the call — we'll be straight with you about whether we're the right fit.

My books are a mess and years behind. Can you still help?

That's a large part of what we do. Cleanup and catch-up is quoted separately from the monthly retainer, based on how many months and transactions are involved. The free health check tells you the scope before you commit to anything.

How do you access my data securely?

Read-only accountant access to QuickBooks first, and only as much as the work requires. We sign an NDA before any access is granted. We do not ask for your banking passwords, and we never need them.

Am I locked into a contract?

No. Retainers are month-to-month with 30 days' notice. Setup and project work is a fixed one-time fee agreed in writing before we start.

What happens in the free Inventory Health Check?

A 30-minute call, then we review whatever you can share — a stock report, a QuickBooks file, even spreadsheets. You get back a short written note listing what's inaccurate, what it's likely costing you, and the order we'd fix it in. Yours to keep, whether or not you hire us.

Who actually does the work?

The two people on this page. Nothing is passed to an anonymous back office. As we grow, any team member added works under the same named account lead you started with.
