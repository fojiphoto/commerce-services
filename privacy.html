Privacy Policy

What this site collects, why, how long we keep it, and how to have it deleted.

Short version: the only personal data this site collects is what you type into the contact form. There is no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking cookie.

What we collect

Only what you submit through the health-check form:

  • Your name and company
  • Your work email, and your phone number if you choose to give one
  • Your country and the accounting software you use
  • The description of your problem that you write in the message field

We do not ask for financial account numbers, card details or banking passwords through this website, and you should never send them to us by email.

Why we use it

Solely to reply to your enquiry and, if you go ahead, to deliver the service. We do not sell, rent or share it for marketing, and we do not add you to a mailing list you did not ask for. The lawful basis is your consent in submitting the form, and our legitimate interest in responding to a business enquiry.

Who else processes it

Form submissions are delivered by Formspree, which passes the message to our email and stores a copy on its own servers; their handling is governed by their privacy policy. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, whose servers log IP addresses for security and abuse prevention. We have no access to those logs.

Where your data is handled

Our team is based in Lahore, Pakistan, so anything you send through this site is read and worked on there. Formspree and GitHub, named above, operate their own servers elsewhere.

If you are in the United Kingdom or the European Economic Area, this means your information leaves that area. Pakistan is not covered by a UK or EU adequacy decision, so we rely on your consent in submitting the form, and we keep what we collect to the minimum needed to answer you. If you would rather not send details through the form, email or call us instead and tell us what you are comfortable sharing.

The video on the home page

The home page shows a still image with a play button. Nothing is requested from YouTube, and no YouTube cookie is set, until you press play. Once you do, the player loads from youtube-nocookie.com and Google’s own privacy policy applies to that playback.

Cookies and analytics

This site sets no cookies of its own and runs no analytics or advertising scripts. That is why you are not asked to accept anything when you arrive.

How long we keep it

Enquiries that do not become clients are deleted within 24 months. Client records are kept for as long as the engagement runs, and afterwards only for the period our professional and tax obligations require.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Email Contact@factorialstudio.com and we will respond within 30 days. If you are in the UK or EU and are not satisfied with our response, you may complain to your national data protection authority.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date below.
